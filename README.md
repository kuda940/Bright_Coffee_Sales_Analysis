# ☕ Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyses sales data from **Bright Coffee Shop** for the period **January to June 2023**.

The purpose of the project is to use Excel-based data analysis and visualisation to understand sales performance, product demand, store performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

The analysis provides business insights that can support better decision-making around sales, product availability, staffing, and store operations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis were to:

- Analyse coffee shop sales performance
- Compare sales across different months
- Identify high- and low-performing periods
- Analyse sales by product category
- Compare performance across store locations
- Identify the busiest periods of the day
- Analyse product demand
- Provide recommendations based on the findings

---

## 📈 Key Analysis

### 📅 Monthly Sales Analysis

The analysis covers **January to June 2023**.

| Month | Number of Sales |
|-------|----------------:|
| January | 9,394 |
| February | 8,579 |
| March | 10,384 |
| April | 10,997 |
| May | 12,402 |
| June | 12,382 |

The highest sales volume was recorded in **May**, while **February** recorded the lowest sales volume during the period analysed.

The average monthly sales volume was approximately **10,690 sales**.

---

## ☕ Product Category Analysis

The analysis examined several product categories, including:

- Coffee
- Tea
- Bakery
- Drinking Chocolate
- Coffee Beans
- Flavours
- Loose Tea
- Branded Products
- Packaged Chocolate

Coffee was the largest product category by sales volume, followed by Tea and Bakery products.

---

## 🏪 Store Performance

The analysis compared the performance of three Bright Coffee Shop locations:

- **Lower Manhattan**
- **Hell's Kitchen**
- **Astoria**

Daily sales revenue figures in the report were approximately:

| Store | Sales Revenue per Day |
|-------|-----------------------:|
| Lower Manhattan | $777.35 |
| Hell's Kitchen | $729.20 |
| Astoria | $698.40 |

Lower Manhattan recorded the highest sales revenue per day among the three locations.

---

## ⏰ Sales by Time of Day

Sales were analysed across four time periods:

- Morning
- Afternoon
- Evening
- Night

The analysis shows that **Morning** generated the highest number of sales, followed by **Afternoon**, while **Night** had considerably lower sales activity.

This information can help management plan staffing levels and inventory according to customer demand.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the analysis:

- ☕ Coffee is the strongest-performing product category.
- 📈 Sales increased significantly from February through May.
- 🏆 May recorded the highest sales volume during the January–June period.
- 📉 February recorded the lowest sales volume.
- 🏪 Lower Manhattan recorded the highest daily sales revenue among the three stores.
- 🌅 Morning was the busiest sales period.
- 🌙 Night recorded significantly lower sales activity.

---

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations could be considered:

1. **Focus on high-performing products**  
   Maintain sufficient stock of coffee and other popular products to avoid missed sales opportunities.

2. **Optimise staffing**  
   Allocate more employees during peak periods, particularly in the morning and afternoon.

3. **Investigate low-performing periods**  
   Promotions and special offers could be introduced during slower periods such as February and nighttime.

4. **Strengthen high-performing locations**  
   Analyse successful practices at Lower Manhattan and consider applying them to other locations.

5. **Use seasonal sales trends**  
   Monthly sales patterns can be used to improve inventory planning, staffing, and promotional campaigns.
